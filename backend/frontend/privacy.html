Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026 · Applies to the Discra web apps and mobile app (alpha).

Who we are

Discra is a delivery-dispatch platform. Your dispatch company (the organization that invited you or created your account) decides what order and delivery data enters Discra and is the controller of that data; Discra processes it on their behalf to run the service.

What we collect

  • Account & profile — name, email, phone, and an optional profile photo, tied to your organization.
  • Orders & deliveries — order details your organization enters or imports, including delivery recipient name, addresses, and contact details.
  • Driver location — while a driver is signed in to the driver app, their device location is shared with their organization's dispatchers to coordinate deliveries. Location pings expire from our systems automatically.
  • Proof of delivery — delivery photos and recipient signatures captured at drop-off, stored securely for your organization's delivery records.
  • Email integration — if your organization's administrator connects a mailbox, we read incoming order emails from it to create orders.
  • Usage & security records — sign-ins and sensitive actions are recorded in an audit trail; operational logs avoid personal content.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service your organization signed up for (account management, dispatch, delivery tracking, proof of delivery, billing seats).
  • To coordinate active deliveries (driver location and status, shared only within your organization).
  • To keep the service secure and accountable (authentication, access control, audit logging).

Who processes it for us

We use these service providers, under their standard data-protection terms:

  • Amazon Web Services — hosting and storage (United States).
  • Stripe — subscription billing for your organization.
  • Google — only if your administrator connects a Gmail mailbox for order intake.
  • Anthropic — order emails may be processed by an AI service to extract order details, when your organization enables email parsing.
  • Routing & maps — delivery coordinates are sent to routing providers (Amazon Location, OpenRouteService) to plan routes; map tiles are loaded from CARTO in your browser.

Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Retention

  • Order, delivery, and proof-of-delivery records are kept while your organization's account is active.
  • Driver location pings and push-notification registrations expire automatically.
  • Backups roll off automatically (35 days for databases, 90 days for prior file versions).

Your rights

You can review and update your profile in the app. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, contact your organization's Discra administrator, who can raise the request with the Discra operator named in your organization's service agreement. We will support access and deletion requests within the limits of your organization's legal record-keeping needs.

Changes

Discra is in an early-access (alpha) phase and this policy will evolve with the product. Material changes will be announced in the app.
